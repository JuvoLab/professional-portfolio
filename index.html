From analysis to a better performance conversation.

This project extends my physiological assessment background into match analysis, data interpretation, and the communication of practical insights.

Look beyond the league table

Compare results with the quality of chances created and conceded to give team outcomes more context.

Recognize changing patterns

Follow rolling and cumulative trends to see what a full-season total can hide.

Focus the next review

Identify periods and performance gaps that deserve closer investigation with match footage and team context.

A practical example: Crystal Palace’s results-to-chance-quality gap shifted from +12.34 after 19 appearances to −1.58 after 38. The useful question is how chance creation, defending, and scoring evolved across those periods—not simply where the team finished.

Scope: descriptive analysis of the 2015/16 season. Dashboard comparisons guide further review; they do not establish causes or describe current team quality.
